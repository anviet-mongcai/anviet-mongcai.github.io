FAQ

Do I need a licence to rent a scooter for Tra Co?

No. Flazz and Evo Grand Lite are electric mopeds under 50 km/h; riders aged 16+ need no licence. Riders 16–17 need parental consent.

How much is a scooter for Tra Co?

Flazz: 4 hours 100,000 VND, full day 160,000 VND. Evo Grand Lite: 4 hours 120,000 VND, full day 180,000 VND. Price per bike, 2 helmets included.

What documents do I need?

An ID card, passport or border pass to check and write into the contract. We do not keep the original.
