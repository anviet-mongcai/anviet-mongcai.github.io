FAQ

How much is the rental?

Price per bike: Flazz 100,000 VND for the 4-hour package, 160,000 VND for a full day (24h); Evo Grand Lite 120,000 VND for 4 hours, 180,000 VND for a full day. Two helmets and delivery within 3 km are included; the full-day package also comes with a charger. For several days, send your pick-up date & time and number of bikes on WhatsApp or Zalo and we quote right away.

Do I need a driving licence?

No. The Flazz and Evo Grand Lite are e-mopeds (design speed up to 50 km/h, motor up to 4 kW). Under Vietnam's 2024 Law on Road Traffic Order and Safety, anyone aged 16 or over may ride this type of vehicle without a licence. Renters aged 16–17 need parental consent.

What documents do I need?

Your passport, Vietnamese ID card or border pass, so we can check it and note it in the contract. We never keep the original.

Where do I pick up the bike? Do you deliver?

Yes. Free delivery within 3 km: hotel, homestay, Bac Luan border gate or bus station. Or collect the bike at 16 Vạn Ninh, Mong Cai ward 1. Return it at the place we agreed.

How far can I ride? Can I go to Tra Co beach?

About 50 km on a full charge, depending on load and riding style. Tra Co – Sa Vi Cape and back is about 30 km and inside the riding area (Mong Cai ward 1). No riding on the sand. Battery low? Call us and we come and charge it for free.

How do I book? Are bikes available?

Send your pick-up date & time, package, number and model of bikes, and delivery place. We check availability and hold the bikes as soon as you confirm. You pay at pick-up.

How do the deposit and payment work?

A deposit is taken under the contract and refunded as soon as you return the bike, within 30 minutes at most. Vietnamese dong only — cash or bank transfer, no foreign currency. We never keep your original documents.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes. Tell us at least 2 hours in advance and you only pay the difference.

What if the bike breaks down?

Call us within 2 hours and do not repair it yourself. For a technical fault we swap the bike for an equivalent one or refund you. If a warning light comes on, stop somewhere safe and call right away.

What if it rains?

Raincoats are available on request — tell us when booking. In rain, never ride through a flooded road, however shallow.

How many people can ride one bike?

Each bike carries 1 passenger at most, and 2 helmets are included. For a bigger group, add more bikes on the same contract.

Can I rent with a border pass?

Yes. Ride only within the area and period your pass allows, no further than Mong Cai wards 1, 2 and 3, and return the bike before leaving Vietnam.
